What is lumic

A streaming platform made for people who live with headphones in.

lumic started with a simple frustration: great music apps punish you the second you go offline. So we built one that doesn't. Stream millions of tracks when you're online, download them in one tap when you're not, and keep listening on the train, on a flight, or wherever your signal disappears. It's the same idea Spotify made popular — just tuned for people who share more, save more, and pay less.

iPhone & iPad

Add lumic to your Home Screen

lumic isn't on the App Store yet — instead, it installs straight from Safari as a full app icon on your Home Screen. Once it's added, it opens full-screen, works offline, and feels exactly like a native app — no App Store, no waiting on review.

Action needed

You're viewing this in an in-app browser (like Instagram, Facebook, or TikTok), and "Add to Home Screen" won't show up here. Tap the ••• or share icon in this app and choose Open in Safari first, then follow the steps below.

Safari · takes 15 seconds
  1. 1Open this page in Safari. Add to Home Screen only works from Safari — not Chrome, and not an app's built-in browser.
  2. 2Tap the Share icon . On iPhone it's in the bottom toolbar; on iPad it's at the top, next to the address bar.
  3. 3Scroll down the share menu and tap Add to Home Screen. If you don't see it, tap Edit Actions at the bottom and add it.
  4. 4Confirm the name reads lumic, then tap Add in the top-right corner.
  5. 5Find the lumic icon on your Home Screen and tap it to open — you're in, no App Store required.
